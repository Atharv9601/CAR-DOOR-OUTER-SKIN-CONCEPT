# CAR DOOR OUTER SKIN CONCEPT 🚗🖊️

## 1. Overview

The **Car Door Outer Skin Concept** project focuses on creating the exterior geometry of a passenger car door using CATIA surface and solid modelling.  
The model captures the full outer door skin and window frame openings, forming a clean base for later structural and trim development.

---

## 2. Aim 🎯

- Develop a **concept outer door panel** with realistic proportions and window frame layout.  
- Use CAD to control the **overall door contour, beltline, and window openings** through parametric sketches and surfaces.  
- Produce a visually clean, portfolio‑ready model that can be thickened and detailed into a manufacturable door later.

---

## 3. Tech Stack & Tools 🧰

### Software

- 3D CAD: **CATIA V5** (Wireframe & Surface Design / Part Design)

### Core Skills

- Sketch and 3D curve creation for automotive side door profiles.  
- Surface generation and refinement for the outer door skin.  
- Solid modelling of thin‑wall panels based on surfaces.  
- Basic model organisation for future BIW integration.

---

## 4. Geometry & Features 🧱

- **Outer door skin**  
  - Main exterior surface representing the visible body side of the door.  
  - Includes a lower taper and character change in the lower area to mimic real door styling.

- **Window frame with two openings**  
  - Integrated upper frame with separate front and rear glass openings, suitable for a rear side door layout.  
  - Geometry prepared for future glass and seal modelling.

- **Continuous beltline and pillar interfaces**  
  - Beltline region defined as a straight, continuous transition between lower body and window frame.  
  - Side edges shaped to connect to adjacent body side panels and pillars.

- **Thin‑wall solid**  
  - Outer surface converted into a thin solid body to represent the stamped panel thickness.  
  - Uniform wall used as a starting point for later flanges and hemming features.

---

## 5. Modelling Workflow 🧩

1. **Profile and outline sketches**  
   - Create 2D sketches defining door overall outline, beltline, and window frame.  
   - Constrain dimensions and angles to keep the concept parametric.

2. **Surface creation**  
   - Generate the outer skin using surface tools (extrudes, sweeps, or multi‑section surfaces) guided by the profiles.  
   - Ensure smooth transitions between the upper window frame region and the lower door skin.

3. **Window frame openings**  
   - Cut out front and rear window openings based on separate sketches.  
   - Refine the frame thickness for realistic proportions.

4. **Solid conversion**  
   - Thicken the final surface model to represent the stamped metal door outer panel.  
   - Check overall shape and continuity in multiple views.

5. **Visualisation**  
   - Capture isometric and side views highlighting the door outline, window frame, and lower taper.  
   - Prepare images for use in portfolio slides and documentation.

---

## 6. Key Features & CAD Highlights 🌟

- **Clean outer skin concept** representing a realistic passenger car door silhouette and glass area.  
- **Parametric window frame layout** ready for adding glass, seals, and hardware in future iterations.  
- **Simple, robust geometry** suitable as a starting point for inner panel, hinge/latch zones, and trim development.  
- **Portfolio‑ready visuals** that clearly show understanding of basic automotive door proportions and surfacing.
